# Open training data and non-AndroidWorld environments for a small screenshot-only Android agent

Scope note: this file catalogs datasets and environments (release date, license, size, platform, apps, annotation types, AndroidWorld (AW) overlap) and collects evidence on which data types move *online* success. Method-level findings (DAgger, GRPO and so on) are in the earlier report `reports/Small GUI agent training methods.md` and are not repeated, except where a dataset or environment result is needed. "AW apps" below means the 20 apps in AW's task modules: Audio Recorder, Chrome, Simple Calendar Pro, Camera, Clock, Contacts, Pro Expense, Files, Markor, OsmAnd, Phone, Broccoli, Retro Music, Simple Draw Pro, Simple Gallery Pro, Simple SMS Messenger, Settings, VLC, Joplin and Tasks ([earlier project notes, from the installed android_world `task_evals`](https://github.com/google-research/android_world)). Licenses come from the dataset card or repo metadata as fetched on 2026-11-18. "No license tag" means the card or repo declares none, which is not the same as permissive.

## Q1. Trajectory datasets: what exists, under what terms, and how much does each overlap with AndroidWorld?

### Takeaway
Clean human trajectory data (AndroidControl, AitW/AitZ, AMEX, GUI-Odyssey, AndroidLab Instruct) is all 2023–2024 vintage. Most of it lacks terminal and answer labels, and we already use most of it. The large 2026 open sets that carry thoughts, recovery and answer/terminate supervision (OpenMobile v1/v2, Uni-GUI-OpenMobile, OS-Genesis) were mostly collected on the AW emulator and AW apps. The exceptions are the **simulator (MobileGym) split of OpenMobile-Data-v2**, which uses Chinese commercial-style simulated apps, and **PhoneWorld**'s mock consumer apps, plus a few third-party sets on non-AW apps.

### Inferences
- The cleanest **new** supervision available for our missing behaviors is: (a) GUI-Reflection's reflection SFT data, which is built on the AC/Odyssey/AMEX images we already have and adds the ineffective-action-then-recover pattern our no-op failures need; (b) the **simulator splits of OpenMobile-Data-v2** (~3.9K trajectories, ~35K steps) with Thought + `answer`/`terminate` on non-AW apps; (c) PhoneWorld's 3,354 verified trajectories, if they are actually downloadable (only APKs are confirmed). Both (b) and (c) are Chinese-language consumer-app UIs, so they teach behavior (answering, terminating, multi-step flows) more than English app vocabulary.
- OpenMobile-Data-v2's *emulator* split can be partially used if filtered by `task.apps`/package. Drop every trajectory that touches an AW app or the "AndroidWorld"/"MiniWoB" support apps, then dedup goals against AW templates. What remains (Amaze, Material Files, MiXplorer, Etar, Omni Notes, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, NewPipe, AntennaPod, Librera, Mattermost…) maps closely onto the productivity categories we need (file managers, calendar, notes, browser). The size of the surviving subset is unknown and must be measured.
- OpenMobile v1, Uni-GUI-OpenMobile, OS-Genesis-mobile and AndroidGen data were collected *on the AW emulator and AW apps*. Under our rule ("no AW tasks or variants") they are not literally AW tasks. However, OpenMobile's nearest neighbours to AW test tasks reach 0.80 cosine, and its own analysis says gains come from covering AW tasks' functionalities. They should be excluded, or used only in a separately reported "AW-app overlap" ablation.
- Aguvis stage-2 offers drop-in thoughts for our AMEX rows, which have blank intents. Its ablation says inner monologue matters mostly for low-level execution (AC-Low 69.1 → 80.5).
- Non-commercial licenses (Mobile3M, CAGUI, UGround, PhoneWorld APKs) matter only if the LFM3 agent is ever shipped commercially.

### Gaps
- Exact AitW, AitZ and AMEX terminal-action vocabularies, and per-dataset screenshot resolutions, were not verified in this pass (only GUI-Odyssey's device list, AndroidControl*/Uni-GUI's 1080×2400 and OpenMobile-2's 999×999 coordinate space were).
- OpenMobile-Data-v2 and OpenMobile v1 carry no license on their HF cards. The terms need to be confirmed with the authors before redistribution or commercial use.
- I could not confirm whether PhoneWorld's 3,354 trajectories (as opposed to the APKs and runner) are released.
- The ScaleCUA Android/iOS trajectory counts and the AndroidGen data size were not found.
- The GUI-Reflection online environment's 11 apps (its Table 21) were not retrieved, so their AW overlap is unknown.

## Q2. Which datasets carry reasoning, reflection/error-recovery, information-retrieval answers and terminal labels, and which of these data types matter for online success?

### Takeaway
The data types with measured *online* gains are: (1) step-level thoughts/CoT, (2) error-recovery segments in which the agent's own mistake stays in the history, (3) environment-grounded synthetic tasks that cover many app functionalities and many apps, and (4) verified-answer and terminate supervision. Volume of clean human demonstrations is not among them. Answer-bearing *trajectory* data on non-AW apps is scarce. It exists mainly in OpenMobile-Data-v2 (simulator), PhoneWorld, MobileGym's AnswerSheet tasks and AndroidLab query tasks, so navigate-then-answer data likely has to be synthesized.

### Cited Findings
**Reasoning/CoT annotations and their online effect**
- AitZ provides human Chain-of-Action-Thought (screen description, action thinking, outcome) for 18,643 screens — [arXiv 2403.02713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02713)
- Aguvis: GPT-4o inner monologue for 35K trajectories. Removing it drops AC-High 61.5 → 60.3, AC-Low 80.5 → 69.1 and ScreenSpot 84.4 → 79.3 (7B). Online AW results with and without monologue were not reported in the extracted ablation — [arXiv 2412.04454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04454)
- Ferret-UI Lite-3B on AW (mean of 5 runs): human episodes only 13.7 → +short CoT 15.8 → +long CoT 19.6 → +synthetic data 5K/13K/17K trajectories 20.3/22.4/25.2. RL then took it from 25 to 28 — [arXiv 2509.26539](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.26539v1)
- GUI-Owl-1.5-8B-Thinking: removing the "unified CoT synthesis" (observation, reflection, memory, progress tracking per step) drops AW from 71.6 to 65.0 and OSWorld from 52.9 to 47.4 — [arXiv 2602.16855](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16855)
- OpenMobile-Data-v2 trains every step as `Thought:` + one tool call — [card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenMobile-2/OpenMobile-Data-v2)

**Reflection / error-recovery data**
- GUI-Reflection builds two kinds of recovery data from successful AndroidControl trajectories. The first modifies the goal so the original action becomes wrong, then labels a reflection action at t+1 (often Back) and a corrected re-attempt at t+2 (17,557 samples). The second inserts an *ineffective* action (scroll at the bottom, tap on a non-interactive element) with an unchanged screenshot and rewrites the next thought to reflect on it (15,394 samples) — [arXiv 2506.08012](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08012)
- GUI-Reflection-8B reaches 34.5% on AW, and online reflection tuning raised its own-environment success to 34.72% (Level-1 tasks reach ~90%, Level-2 29.36% by iteration 3) — [arXiv 2506.08012](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08012)
- OpenMobile's policy-switching data keeps learner errors in the history. The authors report that it "substantially strengthens the agent's error-recovery capability, which in turn drives the downstream performance gains" — [arXiv 2604.15093](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15093)
- AgentNet trajectories include a `reflection` field "generated by reflector for error analysis" (desktop) — [AgentNet card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xlangai/AgentNet)
- GUI-Critic-Train provides pre-action error-diagnosis supervision (mobile + web) — [arXiv 2506.04614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04614)

**Information-retrieval / answer supervision**
- OpenMobile's task generator explicitly produces QA tasks with constrained answer formats ("In the Tasks app, which tasks due this week are not completed yet? Answer with titles only…"), but on AW apps — [arXiv 2604.15093](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15093)
- PhoneWorld tasks define a final answer `y_T` checked by "answer verifiers". 49 of its 120 benchmark tasks are answer-based — [arXiv 2605.29486](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29486)
- MobileGym uses "a structured AnswerSheet protocol that avoids free-text matching failures" — [arXiv 2605.26114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26114)
- MAI-Gym generates cross-app "worlds" from a persona and scenario and then writes tasks "requiring agents to retrieve, reason over, and integrate information across applications" — [arXiv 2610.20939](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20939)
- OpenMobile-2's action space has `answer`, but the paper reports no separate QA data category — [arXiv 2610.14157](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14157)
- AMEX-derived LearnGUI IR tasks end in `TASK_COMPLETE[answer]`, but answers are not scored — [LearnAct](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.13805)

**Terminal labels**
- GUI-Odyssey has COMPLETE and IMPOSSIBLE actions — [card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenGVLab/GUI-Odyssey)
- AndroidControl's normalized action space has no terminate — [AndroidControl*](https://huggingface.co/datasets/UI-MOPD/AndroidControl-Star)
- OpenMobile-2 uses `terminate(success|failure)`, and its RL reward subtracts 0.2 when "the agent claims completion but the verifier fails" — [arXiv 2610.14157](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14157)

**Environment-grounded synthetic data and app/functionality coverage** (also see Q6)
- OpenMobile: "Broad functionality coverage drives agent performance". Tasks whose required functionalities are better covered by synthetic data succeed more — [arXiv 2604.15093](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15093)
- GUI-Owl-1.5: removing virtual-environment trajectories drops in-house Mobile-Eval from 86.7% to 50.0% (8B) — [arXiv 2602.16855](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16855)

### Inferences
- For our failure profile (zero answers, false `complete`, wait/no-op loops), the highest-value data types are, in order: answer-terminated navigate-then-answer episodes, ineffective-action recovery samples (the GUI-Reflection recipe), and terminate-with-verification labels. All three can be made from data we hold (AC, Odyssey, AMEX) plus simulator data, without touching AW.
- GUI-Odyssey's COMPLETE/IMPOSSIBLE labels and OpenMobile-2's terminate(failure) are the only ready-made sources found that teach *giving up/infeasible* rather than only *success*.
- The CoT evidence (Ferret 13.7 → 19.6, GUI-Owl-1.5 65.0 → 71.6) supports keeping our short intent field filled on every row, which means filling AMEX's blanks (Aguvis thoughts are one ready source).

### Gaps
- No public, clean (non-AW-app), English, *answer-terminated* mobile trajectory set at scale was found. AndroidLab's Android Instruct may contain query tasks, but this was not verified.
- No study isolates the AW gain from adding QA trajectories alone at 2–4B.

## Q3. Grounding and perception datasets usable cheaply

### Takeaway
Large, permissively licensed mobile grounding corpora exist: OS-Atlas, Aria-UI, MobileViews, RICO-derived sets and the Aguvis grounding split. UGround and Mobile3M are non-commercial. Earlier project notes found grounding pretraining gives only small agent-level gains when the base already has GUI perception. The newest evidence says grounding data does not hurt navigation and a balanced ratio is best at 3B.

### Cited Findings
- Aguvis grounding split (1.036M single-step samples): SeeClick 271K, GUIEnv 328K, GUIAct 67K, WebUI 57K, **Widget Captioning 101K, RicoSCA 173K, UI RefExp 16K, RICO Icon 16K** (mobile), OmniACT 7K — [arXiv 2412.04454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04454)
- UGround: 10M GUI elements over 1.3M screenshots (mostly web-synthetic). The HF data is **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** — [arXiv 2410.05243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05243); [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/osunlp/UGround-V1-Data)
- OS-Atlas data: Apache-2.0 HF tag (2024-11). Earlier project notes: 13M+ elements, 2.3M screenshots, and +0.5 (4B) / +2.7 (7B) AC-Low step SR from grounding pretraining — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OS-Copilot/OS-Atlas-data); [arXiv 2410.23218](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.23218)
- Aria-UI data and context-aware data: Apache-2.0 (Dec 2024 / Feb 2025) — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Aria-UI/Aria-UI_Data); [arXiv 2412.16256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16256)
- MobileViews: RL grounding on it improves grounding accuracy "by up to 6.1%"; MIT — [arXiv 2409.14337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14337)
- RICO Widget Captioning, Screen2Words and ScreenQA mirrors are tagged CC BY 4.0 — [RICO-WidgetCaptioning](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bevaya/RICO-WidgetCaptioning); [RICO-Screen2Words](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bevaya/RICO-Screen2Words); [RICO-ScreenQA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bevaya/RICO-ScreenQA)
- GUIAct (Mar 2024): Apache-2.0 web + smartphone actions — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yiye2023/GUIAct)
- MONDAY pre-training gives "an average performance gain of 18.11%p on an unseen mobile OS platform" — [arXiv 2505.12632](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12632)
- Ferret-UI Lite-3B SFT mixes GroundUI, OS-Atlas, UGround, Aria-UI, Aguvis, WaveUI, ShowUI, Jedi and AgentNet. "Navigation data provides complementary supervision that strengthens grounding ability, while grounding data does not degrade performance on navigation benchmarks. Balanced ratios achieve the best overall results." — [arXiv 2509.26539](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.26539v1)
- UI-Venus-1.5's 10B-token mid-training corpus (30+ sources incl. Mind2Web, ShowUI, AITW): semantic perception 20.8%, GUI-VQA 22.1%, grounding 24.8%, the rest navigation/reasoning. A teacher-scoring/rewrite loop raised the "high-fidelity" share of navigation traces from 69.7% to 89.7% — [arXiv 2602.09082](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.09082)
- GUI-Reflection's "action verification" (did this action achieve purpose X?) and "action reversal" tasks are perception-style auxiliary data built from AC/Odyssey screen pairs. They lifted its pretrain model's action-verification accuracy from 57.95 to 87.56 — [arXiv 2506.08012](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08012)

### Inferences
- The cheapest perception data aimed at *our* failure modes is not more grounding but **before/after screen-pair tasks** (action verification, "did anything change?", undo action), built from consecutive AC/Odyssey screenshots as GUI-Reflection did. These target no-op detection directly.
- If grounding rows are added, a 2B-scale mix should keep navigation dominant. Ferret's "balanced" finding and UI-Venus's ~25% grounding share are the only ratio evidence found.

### Gaps
- The exact Ferret-UI Lite mixture percentages (its Appendix D figure) were not extracted.
- No 2B-scale ablation linking a grounding dataset to AW success was found.

- **Mac-friendly environments:** MobileGym, MobileGym++ and MAI-Gym's novel apps are browser simulators (Chromium, ~400 MB per instance). They should run on the Mac without Android emulators or KVM, which removes our two-emulator bottleneck for RL or rollouts (inference; not tested). MAI-Gym's Android path needs ReDroid on a Linux host kernel, so it does not suit macOS.
- **Clean on-emulator task sources**, ranked cleanest first:
  1. AndroidLab's five non-AW apps: Bluecoins, Cantook, Maps.me, PiMusic, Zoom.
  2. PhoneWorld APKs (non-commercial; Chinese UIs).
  3. Self-generated tasks on non-AW F-Droid apps in the needed categories, which OpenMobile-2 already installs: Amaze / Material Files / MiXplorer (files), Etar (calendar), Omni Notes FOSS (notes), DuckDuckGo (browser), Librera, AntennaPod.
  4. B-MoCA's 131 simple tasks.
- **Fossify caution (inference):** MobileWorld's "Fossify" calendar/gallery/messages apps are, to my knowledge, forks of the Simple Mobile Tools apps that AW uses (Simple Calendar Pro, Simple Gallery Pro, Simple SMS Messenger). Their UIs are therefore near-identical. Tasks on Fossify apps should be counted as "AW-app overlap", not as clean.
- Under our stricter rule, the norm in the literature (same app, different instruction, plus a similarity report) is weaker than what we want. AndroidWorld-Generalization's +26.1 / +15.7 / +8.3 ladder shows that same-app training inflates AW. For a "fair" comparison with GUI-Owl, report two numbers: no-AW-app training, and AW-app-overlap training. Also run OpenMobile's nearest-neighbour removal ablation.
- AndroidLab and MobileWorld, plus the clean MobileGym test split, are useful *held-out online* checks beside AW. They must not also be used as training pools. GUI-Gym's published eval trajectories on those benchmarks are therefore off-limits too.

### Gaps
- MobileGym's code license and whether its 160 train templates are downloadable separately from OpenMobile-2's release were not confirmed.
- B-MoCA's need for live web/login accounts (Gmail, Instagram, Walmart) was not checked, and live dependencies may make some tasks non-reproducible.
- The AndroidArena task count was not found.
- The AndroidEnv task list was not retrieved.
- MAI-Gym's mocked-app license is unstated; its repo shows no license.

## Q5. Synthetic task generation approaches and their measured value at small scale

### Takeaway
At 3–9B, environment-grounded synthesis plus a verifier gives large online gains: Ferret-UI Lite-3B +5.6 from 17K synthetic trajectories, OS-Genesis-4B 0 → 15.2 with 1K, MobileGym GRPO on a 4B +12.8 in-sim, PhoneWorld app-coverage +10.4 AW points at a fixed budget. The strongest recent recipes generate tasks *jointly with programmatic verifiers* in controllable (often simulated) apps rather than relying on VLM judges.

### Cited Findings
- **Reverse synthesis (OS-Genesis):** explore → turn ⟨pre, action, post⟩ into low-level then high-level tasks. InternVL2-4B AW went 0.00 (zero-shot) → 4.02 (task-driven) → 15.18 (OS-Genesis), using 1K trajectories — [arXiv 2412.19723](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19723) (numbers via earlier project notes). OpenMobile's table lists OS-Genesis at 1.5K trajectories → 17.4 AW on a stronger base, against OpenMobile 2.8K → 64.7 (comparison is "imperfect": different bases) — [arXiv 2604.15093](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15093)
- **Explorer-generated tasks (AutoPlay):** 20K tasks on 20 Android apps and 10K on 13 Ubuntu apps. Up to +20.0% mobile success from SFT, and an additional +5.7% from RL with an MLLM-verifier reward — [arXiv 2509.25047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25047)
- **Global environment memory + compositional/QA tasks (OpenMobile):** 2.8K instructions → Qwen2.5-VL-7B 25.5 → 51.7, Qwen3-VL-8B → 64.7 on AW (AW apps) — [arXiv 2604.15093](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15093)
- **Multi-agent online synthesis + offline task rewriting (Ferret-UI Lite-3B):** AW 19.6 → 20.3 / 22.4 / 25.2 at 5K / 13K / 17K synthetic trajectories — [arXiv 2509.26539](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.26539v1)
- **Declarative templates in a simulator (MobileGym):** 160 train templates, GRPO Qwen3-VL-4B +12.8 pts on 256 held-out test templates — [arXiv 2605.26114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26114)
- **Trace-grounded mock apps + verifier-coupled task synthesis (PhoneWorld):** tasks are generated together with the verifier from the app's content and state schema ("task–environment–verifier closure") — [arXiv 2605.29486](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29486)
- **Persona-driven cross-app worlds + failure-driven generation (MAI-Gym):** coding agents (Claude Code / Codex) diagnose failed rollouts and the generator "composes tasks that exercise the weak capabilities". Verifiers are accepted only after passing execution on initial, completed and reset states — [arXiv 2610.20939](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20939)
- **GUI-Owl-1.5:** DAG-based task synthesis from human-authored workflow graphs, automated rollouts with DAG validation, human demos for unsolved tasks, and "virtual environments via Vibe Coding" for pop-ups/CAPTCHAs — [arXiv 2602.16855](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16855)
- **Other 2025–26 generators (release pending):** GUI-ReWalk (stochastic exploration → intent-aware reasoning, multi-stride cross-app) — [arXiv 2509.15738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15738); SEE (explicit UI transition graph, avg 14.8-step trajectories "while avoiding spurious loops") — [arXiv 2607.18046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18046); M²-Miner (MCTS with Infer/Orchestra/Judge agents, intent recycling) — [arXiv 2602.05429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05429)
- **Video-derived trajectories:** MONDAY (313K frames), WildGUI (12M trajectories), T2T (>1M samples; gains on AndroidControl "without any in-distribution training data") — [MONDAY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12632); [Video2GUI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14747); [T2T](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18715)

### Inferences
- For our setup, the most transferable recipe is MobileGym/PhoneWorld/MAI-Gym-style generation of tasks *with* programmatic checks on apps we control. Use AW's `common_validators`-style adb/SQLite checks we write ourselves, never AW task classes, and include answer tasks with a fixed answer format (AnswerSheet idea). This avoids VLM-judge false positives, which earlier notes flagged as the main risk.
- OpenMobile's own finding (functionality coverage drives AW success) cuts both ways. It explains why AW-app synthesis works so well, and it implies that clean-app synthesis must cover the same *functionality types* (create/edit/delete records, search-then-read, share across apps, settings toggles) to transfer.

### Gaps
- No synthetic-task paper reports results at ≤2B on AW with clean (non-AW) apps. The closest are MobileGym-4B (sim test set) and the PhoneWorld 4B RL run (which also used real apps).

## Q6. Data quantity: when does more static trajectory data stop helping online success, and what mixtures do strong small models use?

### Takeaway
More static demonstrations of the *same kind* show log-linear offline gains and quickly flat online transfer. PhoneWorld's sweep is the clearest evidence: 0 → 36K mock-app steps raised in-domain success 14.2 → 73.3 while AW stayed ~46%. At a *fixed* budget, more apps and more functionality coverage move online numbers, as do adding CoT, recovery data and online RL. Strong small models use a mid-training mixture (perception, VQA, grounding, navigation in roughly equal parts), then offline RL, then online RL, and online RL gives the largest 2B jump.

### Cited Findings
- **HyperTrack (UI-TARS-1.5-7B, 16 → 13,555 episodes):** "Performance scales approximately linearly with the logarithm of training data size" for both SFT and DAPO-style RL. RL ≥ SFT at equal data, with "substantially larger" gaps on unseen apps. Metrics are offline type/exact match — [arXiv 2605.27134](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27134)
- **PhoneWorld volume sweep (Qwen3.5-9B SFT):** adding 0 / 10K / 20K / 36,193 PhoneWorld steps raises PhoneWorld success 14.2 → 64.2 / 70.0 / 73.3, while "AndroidWorld remains near 46%" and AndroidControl moves only 57.9 → 59.3. "More rollouts primarily strengthen in-domain performance, whereas broader app coverage produces the clearest cross-benchmark gains." — [arXiv 2605.29486](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29486)
- **PhoneWorld coverage sweep (fixed 10K steps):** 5 → 34 apps lifts PhoneWorld 46.7 → 65.0 and **AW 61.2 → 71.6**. At a matched 36,193-step budget, 36K AW steps give AW 56.9, while 26K AW + 10K PhoneWorld give 71.6 (+14.7). Note that the baseline itself trains on AW-environment data — [arXiv 2605.29486](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29486)
- **OpenMobile-2 coverage ablation (fixed 2K emulator trajectories):** held-out Pass@1 60.7 → 67.5 as apps go from 15 to 53, while in-domain Pass@1 moves only 73.7 → 71.4 — [arXiv 2610.14157](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14157)
- **OpenMobile-2 overall:** ~106K-step GUI-only SFT takes Qwen3.5-9B AW from 54.3 to 72.8. Hybrid SFT adds little on AW (73.3) and RL adds +0.7 (74.0), but RL adds more on MobileGym (+2.4 SR) — [arXiv 2610.14157](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14157)
- **AndroidControl scaling:** out-of-domain performance "scales significantly more slowly", and "fine-tuning on more data alone may be insufficient for achieving robust out-of-domain performance" for high-level tasks — [arXiv 2406.03679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03679)
- **Small curated sets can match large ones offline:** Magma-R1-3B trained on 2.4K curated samples reaches 88.0 / 75.3 SR on AC-Curated Easy/Hard — [AndroidControl-Curated card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/batwBMW/AndroidControl_Curated); [arXiv 2510.18488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18488)
- **UI-Venus-1.5-2B stage ablation (AW):** mid-training 39.0 → offline RL 45.3 (+6.3) → **online RL 59.8 (+14.5)** → model merge 55.6 (−4.2). Mid-training is 10B tokens from 30+ datasets (perception 20.8%, GUI-VQA 22.1%, grounding 24.8%, remainder navigation/reasoning) — [arXiv 2602.09082](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.09082)
- **Ferret-UI Lite-3B:** CoT and synthetic trajectories gave 13.7 → 25.2 on AW at fixed training steps, and RL 25 → 28. RLVR gains were "most pronounced when the number of SFT steps is smaller" (2K vs 6K vs 10K SFT steps) — [arXiv 2509.26539](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.26539v1)
- **MAI-Gym:** "larger synthesized task pools strengthen the policy" (qualitative in the conclusion) — [arXiv 2610.20939](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20939)
- **MAI-UI:** online RL gains from scaling parallel environments 32 → 512 (+5.2) and step budget 15 → 50 (+4.3) — [arXiv 2512.22047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22047)
- **PhoneWorld mixed RL (separate 4B agent, matched 50-step budget):** SFT 60.3 → real-app RL 77.2 → 50/50 real + mock RL 83.2 on AW — [arXiv 2605.29486](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29486)
- **Aguvis cross-platform:** on MM-Mind2Web, 35K web+mobile trajectories beat 6K web-only (58.5 vs 53.1 cross-task). Mixing platforms helps when the action space is unified — [arXiv 2412.04454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04454)

### Inferences
- For LFM3-2B, adding more AC-style human trajectories (or more AitW) is unlikely to move AW. Our AC/Odyssey/AMEX/ScreenQA mix already reached the regime where offline metrics rise and AW does not, mirroring PhoneWorld's "volume improves in-domain, coverage improves transfer".
- The marginal data budget is better spent on four things:
  1. More distinct apps and functionalities, even at small per-app counts. OpenMobile-2's 2K trajectories over 53 apps beat the same budget over 15 apps.
  2. Answer/terminate/recovery-bearing trajectories.
  3. Thoughts on every row.
  4. A small amount of on-policy data from verifiable environments.
- At our scale (≈1,250 steps × 32 samples), a realistic mix following the cited small-model recipes might be roughly: navigation trajectories ~50–60% (of which a meaningful share is clean environment-grounded, answer/terminate-bearing, multi-app data), grounding/perception ~20–25%, and screen-pair/QA ~20%. This is my inference from UI-Venus's mid-training shares and Ferret's "balanced" result, not a published 2B recipe.
- The −4.2 merge result for UI-Venus-1.5-2B and Ferret's "RL helps most when SFT is short" both suggest small models are sensitive to late-stage mixing. One combined SFT run with a good mix is preferable to separate specialist runs plus merging.

### Gaps
- No published experiment reports AW success vs. number of static human trajectories at ≤3B. The online-transfer evidence is at 4–9B (PhoneWorld, OpenMobile-2) and offline-only at 7–8B (HyperTrack).
- GUI-Owl-1.5-2B's and MAI-UI-2B's exact SFT mixtures and sizes are not disclosed in the extracted text, so the mixture of the model we are trying to beat is unknown.
- The Ferret-UI Lite Appendix D percentages and GUI-Owl-1.5's per-platform data counts were not extracted.
