**Independent dataset-acquisition review — 16 November 2026**

Recommendation: build an **80,000-row research v2** from 56,000 existing AndroidControl rows, 10,000 filtered GUI-Odyssey rows, 8,000 filtered AMEX trajectory rows, and 6,000 ScreenQA rows. Do not add six large datasets indiscriminately. Several recommendations are repackagings of the same screenshots, several require lossy action conversion, and excluding OS-Atlas's `aw_mobile.json` alone does not remove AndroidWorld-app overlap.

**Commercial clearance is unresolved even for the existing baseline.** The official AndroidControl datasheet says both “non-commercial purposes only” and “Apache License, Version 2.0” on printed page 4. That conflict needs a licensor clarification before describing an AndroidControl-trained model as commercially cleared. It also follows AndroidControl into derived collections. This is a conflict between primary statements, not a conclusion that Apache itself prohibits commercial use. [Official datasheet, distribution section](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/a79f3ef3b445fd4659f44648f7ea8ffd-Supplemental-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.pdf), [official release](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/android_control).

This audit inspected the project's action format and split files, public repository trees, dataset cards, papers, licence text, complete small annotation/index files, and a few image payloads. It did **not** download or validate entire screenshot archives, train models, or measure benchmark improvements. Sizes below are decimal GB, compressed transfer sizes unless stated otherwise. Counts marked “publisher” are not independently reconstructed from all media. Effort estimates are my estimates for a competent engineer, including conversion and a small visual QA pass, excluding transfer time and training. “Value AC / AW / small” is an informed qualitative estimate of *incremental* value for AndroidControl, uncontaminated AndroidWorld evaluation, and a 450M–2B model; it is not a measured gain.

The cutoff is **2026-11-16**, not the unelapsed remainder of November. Downloaded payloads total **351,726,766 bytes, approximately 352 MB**, including failed partial transfers, below the 500 MB limit. All downloaded files are in `datasets_scratch/`; [the ledger](datasets_scratch/downloads.jsonl) records URLs, byte counts, HTTP results and hashes. No pre-existing project source, split, or training-data file was edited by this review. Source-repository changes made concurrently by other work are outside this audit.

**How the licence conclusions were made.** These are conclusions about the grants located, not guarantees that every app screenshot is free of third-party rights. Dataset licences, code licences, pretrained-weight licences, and the legal treatment of trained weights are separate questions. None of the inspected permissive licences requires publishing fine-tuned weights merely because the dataset was used; none resolves a separate base-model licence. For model redistribution, retain a source/attribution manifest, licence links and modification notices where applicable, and avoid embedding raw dataset examples in the release without checking the relevant data terms.

**1. GUI-Odyssey — viable new trajectories, with a real conversion cost.** The current release is [HF `hflqf88888/GUIOdyssey`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hflqf88888/GUIOdyssey/tree/main). Its complete `all_annot.json` index is 2,862,103 bytes and contains **8,334 episodes / 127,893 indexed steps**. The older [OpenGVLab repository card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenGVLab/GUI-Odyssey) describes **7,735 episodes, 201 apps and 118,791 screenshots** and points to the new release; these are versions of a dataset, not two independent corpora. The newer paper reports 212 apps; raw app-label aliases are not a reliable count of distinct packages. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.08451), [local index audit](datasets_scratch/odyssey_audit.json).

Images are a split ZIP, `screenshots/screenshots.z01` through `.z08` plus `screenshots.zip`, **92,639,510,437 bytes** in total. Annotation JSONs are separately accessible. A ranged sample yielded a real 2208×1840 PNG. Indexed device dimensions include 720×1280, 1080×2400, 1344×2992, 1440×3120, 2560×1600 and both orientations of 2208×1840. Full archive CRC/completeness remains unverified. Raw download works; do not assume `load_dataset()` works from the card's configuration. The downloaded [`splits/random_split.json`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hflqf88888/GUIOdyssey/blob/main/splits/random_split.json) separates **6,668 train episodes / 102,086 steps** and **1,666 test episodes / 25,807 steps**; use its train IDs for the proposed quota. Other app/task/device splits exist; they are alternate partitions, not extra data. The device split holds out 1,381 tablet episodes. [Split/index join](datasets_scratch/odyssey_split_audit.json).

Three complete episode files were inspected. Actions include `CLICK`, `LONG_PRESS`, `TEXT`, `SCROLL`, `COMPLETE`, `INCOMPLETE`, and special-key click payloads such as `KEY_HOME`, `KEY_BACK`, `KEY_APPSELECT`. The actual sample uses **`TEXT`**, so an adapter accepting only a README's `TYPE` spelling would lose examples. Points are normalized 0–1000; scrolls contain start/end points and sometimes a gesture path. Point/text/back/home map naturally. Recents/app-selector has no matching action. General gestures, container selection and travel distance cannot be represented losslessly by the project's direction-only scroll. An incomplete attempt is not automatically proof that the user's task is infeasible. There are no substantive screen-QA answer labels in the inspected trajectory schema.

Collection used its own Android emulators; downloaded [episode metadata](datasets_scratch/odyssey_example3.json) records Android 14/API 34, rather than the AW emulator configuration. The full index has app **names**, not package IDs. No exact names of the 14 targets were found, but **8 episodes** mention `Simple Calendar` or `Simple Calendar - easy planner`; quarantine these until their packages are identified. The generic `Calendar` label appears in 421 episodes and needs package resolution or conservative exclusion too; it cannot certify an AW-clean calendar source. Home-screen icons and intermediate unlisted apps are beyond this index check.

Human demonstrations are stronger evidence than the subsequently generated GPT-4o instructions, context and SAM2 boxes. Do not use post-hoc context as causal history: an inspected record describes text already entered before the corresponding `TEXT` action. [Issue 19](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/GUI-Odyssey/issues/19) reports 780/25,807 checked cases with dimension-order differences; the author explains that device metadata can differ from rotated screenshots and that normalized coordinates use actual screenshots. That is **not** a demonstrated 3% coordinate-error rate. Older [missing-image reports](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/GUI-Odyssey/issues/16) justify a manifest/CRC check but should not be attributed automatically to the new archive.

The dataset card explicitly declares CC BY 4.0. **USE-WITH-FILTER**: new version only; reconcile actual image orientation; exclude unresolved AW-app labels; retain only supported actions/gestures; review incomplete labels; reconstruct previous actions causally; keep original touch points instead of replacing them blindly with generated box centres. Prefer phone-resolution tasks and short visible-state decisions for 450M models. Cross-app transfer is promising for AW, but tasks requiring memory beyond the current screenshot and last eight text-history entries need rejection or explicit history availability. Expected effort **12–20 h**, more if selective remote-ZIP acquisition is built. Full download at 100 Mbps has an ideal lower bound of about **2.1 h**, excluding overhead.

**2. AMEX — prioritize its 38,709 actual action rows, not its headline element count.** [HF `Yuxiang007/AMEX`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Yuxiang007/AMEX/tree/main) is public. `AMEX/instruction_anno.zip` is **3,105,724 bytes** and was downloaded in full. It contains **3,046 episodes / 38,709 steps**. Its eight image parts plus final ZIP total **93,481,307,584 bytes**; `element_anno.zip` is another **115,510,591 bytes**. The publisher's ~104K screenshots and 110 apps include additional autonomous screen exploration, not 104K demonstrated agent decisions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.17490), [complete annotation audit](datasets_scratch/amex_audit.json).

| AMEX released action | Count | Conversion |
|---|---:|---|
| TAP | 24,815 | `click`, pixel `[x,y]` normalized using actual image dimensions |
| SWIPE | 7,628 | Only validated cardinal page scrolling fits; endpoints/distance are lost |
| TYPE | 2,419 | `input_text`; preserve its separation from Enter |
| TASK_COMPLETE | 2,828 | `status complete` on the supplied observation |
| TASK_IMPOSSIBLE | 220 | `status infeasible` only after reviewing the failure reason/context |
| PRESS_ENTER | 651 | `keyboard_enter` — useful coverage missing from many corpora |
| PRESS_BACK / PRESS_HOME | 135 / 13 | Direct mappings |

No long-press action occurs in this released trajectory annotation set. The schema includes `instruction`, `episode_id`, `steps`, `touch_coord`, `lift_coord`, `device_dim`, `package_name`, `type_text`, `need_human_check`, `interest_region`, and `image_path`. Resolutions span 1080×2280, 1080×2400, 1440×2960/3040/3120 and a small landscape tail; a real sampled PNG is 1440×2960. The paper describes AVD/Genymotion collection with Appium, not AW task collection. Scanning **all 38,709 step package fields found zero exact matches to the 14 AW packages**. This finding covers trajectories, not every autonomous screenshot or home-screen icon. `com.google.android.apps.tasks` is not AW's `org.tasks`.

There are **77 `need_human_check` steps in 56 episodes**. Two episodes contain duplicate/conflicting terminal actions: `f284f97becb6477d9aa9731295908d18` has two impossible markers; `63eeb12752a6426abeb129b8049d5bdd` has impossible followed by complete. Exclude these episodes unless manually repaired. The paper reports over 97% accuracy for functionality descriptions after its verification process; this is not a measured error rate for all action labels. It reports sizeable AndroidControl gains in its own 7B training experiments, with smaller gains under equal-row replacement; do not transfer those gains numerically to a 450M–2B model.

The released functionality coverage is materially smaller than a naive reading of the headline ~711K element count. In [the author's issue response](https://github.com/yuxiangchai/AMEX-codebase/issues/2), the author confirms roughly **300K functionality descriptions on ~20K screens** and says this is the full version. Many elements have empty descriptions. Code availability and its licence do not change the data's **CC BY 4.0** card declaration.

**USE-WITH-FILTER**: exclude flagged/inconsistent episodes, preserve separate Enter, filter gesture meaning, reject invalid/missing screenshots and unresolved impossible cases, then balance apps and actions. Stronger incremental value than another large pile of synthetic boxes, particularly for typing/Enter/terminal behavior. Estimated **10–18 h**; full media transfer about **2.1 h at an ideal 100 Mbps**. Reuse this image cache if testing Aria or Atlas AMEX labels.

**3. GUI-Actor AndroidControl bbox — clean official-train IDs, weak incremental acquisition value.** The exact files are [`cckevinn/GUI-Actor-Data/androidcontrol_bbox.json`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cckevinn/GUI-Actor-Data/blob/main/androidcontrol_bbox.json), **25,035,159 bytes**, and `AndroidControl_images.zip`, **49,306,113,518 bytes**. The full JSON contains **39,701 unique image rows**, not the paper's rounded 47K description. All recoverable IDs are official train, as shown above. Existing local AC screenshots can supply these same images, so downloading the ZIP is unnecessary.

Each row contains an image path, a human referring instruction, an assistant `pyautogui.click(x=..., y=...)` response with unit-interval coordinates, and normalized `bbox_gt` XYXY. All 39,701 responses use click. There are no trajectory histories, scrolls, terminals or answers. Converting a point to the 0–1000 grid is straightforward; converting an entire box into one point discards the box supervision. A normal point-output fine-tune also does not reproduce GUI-Actor's coordinate-free attention architecture. I found no invalid normalized boxes in the scan, but **400 boxes cover more than half the screen**; inspect them rather than treating large regions as precise targets. [Project and training configuration](https://github.com/microsoft/GUI-Actor), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03143).

The HF card has no explicit data licence or licence file. The linked Microsoft code is MIT; original AC terms are conflicting. The JSON has no app/package fields, so apply the project's existing AW exclusion **after joining back to source episodes**, not merely the train-ID allowlist. **REJECT as an additional v2 source**: it adds neither new screenshots nor full-action supervision, and commercial annotation rights are unresolved. If grounding ablation is needed, reweight original AC low-level click rows first. A later licensed, filtered Actor alternative costs **3–6 h** and no new image download; it should replace, not duplicate, those rows.

The other components predate or are independent of AW collection, but the package joins above prove residual VLC/OsmAnd overlap. UIBert sample imagery is 1080×1920; Rico is mixed original Android dimensions and AMEX retains its higher-resolution source family. Do not assume one fixed resolution from an archive's name.

These are grounding records, using normalized XYXY boxes and referring text, with either nested image/element records or flat references. They have **no demonstrated agent action sequence or terminal/answer semantics**. Reference text can describe a noninteractive label or an operation such as swiping, so turning every box into a click is not lossless. **USE-WITH-FILTER** as a small grounding alternative: remove both AW files, join Rico IDs to packages, enforce source train splits where recoverable, exclude noninteractive/ambiguous targets, select one paraphrase per image/box, and deduplicate against AMEX/Aria/original Widget Captioning. No blanket lossless-conversion or commercially cleared-all-sources claim is justified. Budget **6–12 h**; Rico/UIBert-only media is **7.04 GB**, around nine minutes at ideal 100 Mbps, versus ~2.2 h for all non-AW mobile media/labels. Default mix allocation is zero until a grounding deficit is measured.

**5. Aria-UI mobile — a synthetic label expansion of AMEX.** The file is [`Aria-UI/Aria-UI_Data/mobile/aria_ui_mobile_AMEX_1077k.json`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Aria-UI/Aria-UI_Data/tree/main/mobile), **1,632,415,539 bytes**. The publisher describes ~104K AMEX screens and ~1.1M instructions; the filename suggests 1,077K. I inspected a 1 MB prefix containing 47 complete image records, **not all million instructions**. The mobile folder does not provide a new mobile screenshot archive: retrieve the corresponding AMEX images, potentially another 93.48 GB if uncached.

Records contain `image_path`, `clickable_elements`, pixel `bbox`, 0–1000 `bbox_norm`, XML descriptions, functionality text, long/short element captions and multiple instructions. This is grounding supervision, not multi-step demonstrations. A first sampled MyObservatory element has XML text “Load URL” while generated text interprets it as browser forward history: a concrete **semantic disagreement**, not a visually verified error rate. Other generated requests involve copying or entering URLs and cannot automatically become a one-click action. Select only unambiguous, visible, genuinely actionable references and one short phrasing per target.

The Apache-labelled new annotations retain AMEX image obligations. No AW package fields are present in the inspected Aria records; join back to original AMEX metadata, and do not extend the trajectory-only zero-overlap result to all 104K screens. The [paper's 44.8% AW result](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16256) uses a GPT-4o planner and an Aria grounder with history; it is not the performance of a small standalone screenshot-only policy. The card's warnings about broken older desktop image mappings concern desktop files, not a demonstrated defect in this mobile file.

**USE-WITH-FILTER** for a tightly bounded grounding ablation, not a million-row addition. Estimated **6–12 h** once AMEX is cached; annotation-only transfer roughly two minutes at ideal 100 Mbps. Default allocation **zero** because the recommended mix already uses AMEX and because original AC provides grounding supervision. If selected, replace 2–4K rows, rather than counting AMEX, Atlas-AMEX and Aria-AMEX as three diverse sources.

A ranged gzip sample contained a real 1080×1920 JPEG. The [official Rico download page](https://www.interactionmining.org/archive/rico) supplies the metadata and original terms. **Conversion requirements that determine whether the recommendation is viable.** None of these full trajectory corpora can honestly be promised as wholly lossless under the current action space. The viable subset can preserve next-action intent, while explicitly discarding unsupported cases:

1. **Use the actual screenshot frame.** Pixel points need the project's endpoint convention, approximately `round(1000*x/(width-1))`, and the analogous y formula; pre-normalized sources need their own documented convention. The 0–1000 integer grid itself quantizes points. Check for out-of-range coordinates rather than silently clamping corrupt labels. Uniform resizing preserves normalized position; cropping, letterboxing or rotation does not unless accounted for.
2. **Invert finger movement into revealed-content direction.** For ordinary page scrolling, a finger swipe upward usually means project `scroll down`. Horizontal finger-left usually reveals content to the right. Reject drags, drawing, slider movements, pinch/multitouch, diagonal gestures and region-specific scrolls that the fixed executor cannot reproduce. Even accepted cardinal swipes lose length; do not call this exact gesture reproduction.
3. **Preserve observation/action causality.** Build history from previous actions only, capped at the existing eight entries. Do not put future text, generated hindsight state, target bboxes, XML or accessibility content into model inputs. If a source action cannot be represented, reject the dependent remainder or whole episode; do not silently splice across an omitted action. Sampling training rows need not erase the real earlier history used to construct each row.
4. **Keep actions separate.** A focused-field `TYPE` is not click-plus-type or automatic Enter. Do not split a combined focus-and-write label into two decisions while reusing the same screenshot for both: the intermediate observation is missing. Map genuine completion/infeasibility labels to status, not `answer`; map factual screen answers to `answer`. Do not append a terminal action to the final pre-action image merely because the recording ends.
5. **Treat static grounding as an auxiliary task.** A target rectangle is not evidence that clicking it is correct. Require an interactive target and a query whose semantics are satisfied by the click. Keep one expression per image/target in the sampling pool and reuse original screenshot IDs to prevent duplicate weighting across repackagings.
6. **Validate the actual small-model view.** The local `MAX_PIXELS = 880*880` and 32-pixel factor are not a universal hard 720-token limit; the code comment itself says approximately 756. Resize/tokenize with each model's processor and enforce the desired budget explicitly before selecting readable samples. Check tiny text and small targets at that rendered size, especially tablets and 1440-wide Chinese screens. No code was changed by this audit.

For implementation QA, save source repo revision, original file hash, source episode/screen ID, source split, package/app evidence, raw action and conversion rule per selected row. Reject unknown AC source IDs; require every AC-derived row to be in official train and outside the existing AW filter. For independently collected sources, source/package provenance and a later exact-image/perceptual-hash check against frozen test media are complementary. AW app-family exclusion is deliberately stricter than merely removing exact benchmark tasks. Keep dev/test media out of training, and do not tune this mix on the frozen AC test or the 116 AW outcomes.

**Recommended v2 allocation.** This is an **80K total-row replacement mix**, not 80K additions to the existing ~99K. Local `results/data/dataset_verification.json` records a historical VM build of 99,216 rows: 74,595 high-level decisions, 13,573 terminals and 11,048 duplicated low-level rows over 13,573 episodes. These are acquisition/sampling targets, not claims that those exact counts have already survived every visual filter. If fewer rare-action rows survive, use other supported AMEX decisions; never duplicate questionable labels to hit a quota. Keep examples from the same episode/image together when defining any new-source development holdout. Build full causal histories first, then select rows. The quota includes terminal examples, so do not add another terminal copy afterward.

For a **450M grounding ablation**, replace 2–4K of the 50K AC high-level rows with original permitted AC low-level click/long-press rows, or one licensed and filtered UIBert/Widget/Aria source. Keep the total 80K. This is a hypothesis worth testing on validation, not an assumption that a base model's ScreenSpot result makes grounding irrelevant. Do not introduce long synthetic reasoning: use short action intents, normally a sentence, without unsupported hindsight.

For **lower acquisition cost**, replace the 8K AMEX block with filtered GUIAct smartphone decisions, retaining its Enter cases as `keyboard_enter`. Full fresh image acquisition falls from roughly **193 GB** for Odyssey + AMEX + Rico to roughly **103 GB** for Odyssey + GUIAct + Rico, plus annotations. Resolving GUIAct package provenance is a condition of that cheaper variant. Conversely, if cross-app histories cannot be represented faithfully, replace part of Odyssey with more independently selected AMEX decisions rather than accepting hidden-state shortcuts.

The default full-source acquisition is about **4.3 h at an ideal 100 Mbps** or **26 minutes at 1 Gbps**, before throttling, extraction and checks. Real elapsed time can be much longer. These are transfer estimates, not purchased cloud egress quotes; provider charges depend on the chosen host. Budget disk for both archives and extracted images, and measure extraction expansion rather than assuming compressed size equals disk usage. Reusing existing AC images avoids another ~50 GB. A practical engineering allowance for the default ingestion, shared filters and spot QA is **roughly 35–55 engineer-hours**.

At the supplied **one training sample/second** for 2B, one pass costs **16.7 h for 60K**, **22.2 h for 80K**, or **27.8 h for 100K** before evaluation/checkpoint overhead. Two passes double that; training all four model variants requires separate runs. Actual throughput changes with model processor, sequence length and answer length, so 450M speed should be measured, not assumed. An 80K mixed run and an equal-budget AC-only control are more informative than four unpaired source additions. Commercial release remains conditional on resolving the AC terms and documenting the chosen source/media rights; the row mix does not fix a licence conflict.

**What was wrong or overstated in the recommendation.**

- **“These all convert losslessly” is wrong.** Raw gestures, combined writes, bbox tasks, multi-image histories and terminal markers have distinct semantics. The current fixed action format cannot preserve every field or interaction.
- **“Exclude `aw_mobile.json` and Atlas is AW-clean” is wrong.** Its AW media archive must also be excluded, and full Rico joins still find VLC/OsmAnd references in UIBert, Widget Captioning and RICOSCA.
- **GUI-Actor should not be rejected for a demonstrated AC-test leak.** Its inspected file has zero such overlap. Its problems here are duplicate existing train data, limited action coverage and unresolved data terms. The paper's rounded count is not the current file's 39,701 rows.
- **Aria mobile is not a million new mobile screens or trajectories.** It expands AMEX labels and needs AMEX media. Adding Aria, AMEX and Atlas-AMEX without deduplication exaggerates diversity.
- **AMEX's element headline is not the count of populated functionality descriptions or demonstrated actions.** The release has 38,709 trajectory steps and the author confirms a smaller functionality-description subset.
- **“There are no public answer examples” is wrong.** ScreenQA has real answer labels. GUIAct's `answer` field is different: it contains 7,236 complete and 10 impossible markers, not factual answers. That does not make GUIAct useless for trajectory training.
- **All Widget Captioning rows are not usable screenshot examples.** The 12,960 `nr` rows have no public screenshots. ScreenQA short/full answers and Screen2Words' five summaries also must not be counted as independent screens.
- **A permissive code licence does not clear all data or resulting model rights.** AndroidControl has an explicit primary-source conflict; GUI-Actor and MoIT lack an identified standalone data grant; Screen2Words lacks an identified dataset licence; Rico has its own media terms.
- **The search should include actual 2026 releases.** CMGUI and GUI-Libra are real candidates. Samsung's MoIT is downloadable, although not commercially cleared by an identified licence. UI-S1 is downloadable but unsafe for this frozen-test setup without restored provenance. Model-only UI-TARS/Ferret releases must not be advertised as downloadable full training corpora.
- **Large downloads are a practical cost, not proof of nonviability.** Odyssey and AMEX merit small sampled row quotas despite large monolithic media; AitW has smaller subsets; GUIAct is a much cheaper source of Enter/trajectory supervision. The decisive selection criteria are provenance, semantic fit, incremental coverage and label quality within the fixed row budget.

The supporting downloaded source files, complete small-file audits and byte ledger remain in [datasets_scratch](datasets_scratch/). Findings above distinguish publisher claims, exact annotation counts, bounded samples, unresolved licences and estimated benefits so that acquisition can proceed without turning an assumption into a benchmark-cleanliness or deployment claim.
