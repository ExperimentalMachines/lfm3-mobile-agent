**B1 — the new submit predicate erases real actions (training blocker).** `data/build_v2_mix.py:132–139` matches `keyboard` anywhere in the model-generated instruction, OR a bottom-right point. The Odyssey loop at lines 351–357 consumes that tap before intent consistency filtering. Raw adjacency and the second-tap cutoff work; they do not establish that the first tap is a submit. The actual-loop fixture `TEXT → “Tap to hide the keyboard” → result tap` wrongly consumes the hide action. The real executor probe confirms that the replacement `input_text` executes `type_text` plus one Enter, not an arbitrary toolbar or back-button action.

The strongest published counterexample is **train/odyssey/5994133452263654/31**. Source step 26 types `Narcissus` into WPS. Step 27 taps **[686,499]**, described as an icon in the toolbar “above the keyboard”; it opens image selection. The classifier merges it. The published step-31 image shows the Download image picker with the Narcissus photo selected, while its retained source history is **22,23,24,25,26,28,29,30**: text entry is followed directly by controls inside the image picker, with no action opening it. Pressing Enter in the document is not that missing toolbar tap. The [pinned published picker](data_v2_cache/verify3_836e401958/visual/merge_04.png), [saved row/raw-step evidence](data_v2_cache/verify3_836e401958/blocking_examples.json), and [cached earlier step-28 picker image](data_v2_cache/verify3_836e401958/visual/old_wps_after_toolbar.png) make the omission reviewable. The last image is explicitly corroboration from audit 1, not a newly downloaded third-revision row.

Additional overmatches needing review include **4912446279173881/4**, a tap at **[249,453]** consumed on “suggestion … keyboard” wording: the [published next observation, step 5](data_v2_cache/verify3_836e401958/visual/merge_06.png), is an opened **Merck** article, while history has only typed `Zoom`. The intervening tap is gone; whether Enter could independently open that article was not device-tested. **6951686067814574/4**, explicitly “Minimize the keyboard” at **[118,971]**, is also consumed before the [published Generate action](data_v2_cache/verify3_836e401958/visual/merge_05.png). Keyboard hiding is not established as equivalent to Enter. These are supporting cases; the WPS missing image-picker action is the decisive example. A cached Simplenote back-button image likewise confirms that a copied instruction mentioning keyboard can consume a navigation tap.

Among 2,978 reconstructed Odyssey annotation files, **1,858** submit merges were encountered; **108** were outside the bottom-right corner and matched on instruction text alone, with published later rows for **39** such events. **These are candidate counts, not 108 proven errors or an unbiased error-rate estimate.** The WPS example already demonstrates a generator defect affecting retained training history. Tighten submit recognition to positive submit evidence; do not equate mentions of a keyboard, its toolbar, suggestions or dismissal with Enter. Where equivalence is ambiguous, cut the episode rather than deleting a real action and retaining its suffix. [Text-only merge inventory](data_v2_cache/verify3_836e401958/text_only_merges.json).

**Training budget and final gate.** Both jobs preserve 1,250 optimizer steps, batch size 8, accumulation 4, LR `2e-4`, LoRA rank 64/alpha 128 and 774,400-pixel budget. On one GPU this is **40,000 row presentations**, approximately **0.504 passes** through this mix. Uniform sampling gives expected presentations of approximately **28,191 AC, 5,023 Odyssey, 3,764 AMEX and 3,021 ScreenQA**; the 13 Enter rows yield only **6.6 expected presentations**. No epoch or row-budget compensation is warranted. Equal steps/rows does not establish equal token compute or accuracy.

The pin propagation and both templates pass the scoped checks. No GPU training, on-device replay or downstream accuracy claim follows from this audit. Resolve **B1 (false submit merges)** and **B2 (control/selection drags mapped to scroll)**, preserve valid histories by cutting dependent suffixes where needed, rebuild and pin the resulting bytes. Fixing the two isolated random-source labels is useful cleanup, but it is not a demand for noise-free SFT data. **Final verdict for this exact revision and v1 recipe: LFM3-VL-2B — NO-GO; Qwen3.5-2B — NO-GO.**
