**Success validity and power limits**

One new proposal is described above; 87 have no sufficient contradiction in the saved evidence. Sample size alone does not determine power. Using the observed between-template variance **as a planning approximation held fixed**, the cluster SE is **3.247 pp**, giving an approximately **9.10 pp** effect for 80% two-sided superiority power, and **56.6%** NI power at a true difference of zero with this −6.9 pp margin and the lower two-sided 95% bound rule. This is not an exact sign-flip power calculation or additional evidence for the observed effect; [calculation](power_approximation.json). Three seeds give 348 paired outcomes, not 348 independent template replications. The supplied shifted-sign-flip CI is nominal and assumes symmetry; pooled Wilson intervals are ordinary binomial summaries, not cluster-adjusted intervals. The supported claim is the stated conditional NI result in this harness, with rerun/state/checker caveats, not equivalence, superiority over GUI-Owl, or demonstrated NI on every task subset.
