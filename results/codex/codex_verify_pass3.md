**Qwen thinking-output validity still blocks a trustworthy deployment-policy score.**

The real Jinja prompt for thinking-enabled FT ends with `<think>\n`. [generate.py:123](/Users/alpha/lfm3-android/eval/androidcontrol/generate.py:123) saves only tokens after the input. The separate AW removal of the `reasoning_content` fallback is verified and should be retained.
